Visitors

A visitor is one browser, recognised by a random cookie the viewer gives it. The same person on a phone and a laptop counts twice; a private window or cleared cookies looks new. "New" = first seen in the chosen period. Device and browser are read from the user agent and can be wrong. No location lookup is done (that would send addresses to an outside service).
